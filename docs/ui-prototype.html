open-plaud — 3-button UX prototype

Buttons sit on the top edge, so each button's label is shown along the top of the screen, right under the physical button. Labels are context-sensitive (they change per state).

Proposed button map

Physical (top edge)Idle / ReadyWhile recordingWhile syncing
Left⏻ Power (hold = sleep)✕ Cancel (discard)—
Middle↻ Sync—✕ Cancel

Record is now a toggle: press Right to start, press Right again to stop — no holding. Stop saves the clip and queues it; it does not auto-upload. Uploads happen when you press Sync, automatically on boot if Wi-Fi is up, or via the Mac client.

Mac client (companion) — concept

For when the device isn't on Wi-Fi: plug it into the Mac, and the client pulls clips off the device, uploads them to your Railway server, and manages settings (Wi-Fi, server URL, token).
